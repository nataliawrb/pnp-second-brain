# Trust & Calibration Metrics

When outcome metrics alone aren't enough: acceptance rate of AI recommendations, override/correction rate, and escalation-to-human rate catch the case where an AI feature looks efficient but is quietly being overridden or ignored.

Two traps to watch for:
- **Survivorship bias** — the AI cherry-picks easy cases and escalates hard ones; measure across the whole population, not just what the AI chose to handle.
- **Quality substitution** — faster only counts as a win if outcome quality holds; otherwise the cost just moved somewhere less visible.
