# Enablement & Training

The function doesn't scale by the analyst doing more work faster — it scales by everyone else needing the analyst less for the same outcome.

## Training & user access
Structured sessions plus the right level of self-serve access — training without access just creates frustration; access without training just creates wrong numbers confidently reported.

## Self-serve enablement
The tooling, documentation, and dashboard standards that let a PM or designer answer their own question correctly, most of the time, without opening a ticket.

## Team learning, development & growth
Building analytics literacy as a standing capability across product, not a one-off workshop — the difference between a training that happened once and a culture that keeps teaching itself.

## Skill codification
Turning what a good analyst does by instinct into something explicit enough to hand to someone else — a checklist, a framework, a Claude skill. This is the same move as [`knowledge-hub-second-brain.md`](knowledge-hub-second-brain.md), applied to *how to think*, not just *what's been found*.
